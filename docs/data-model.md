# data-model

## core-entities

`_actuals` keys each official selection by Overall in column C and player in column E. `Players-Draft Tracker` keys projections by Player in column A. Its header order as of 2026-08-28 is Player, Proj Pick, Act Pick, Value vs Rank, Board Rank, Pos, NFL Grade, Proj Team, Act Team, Proj Round, Act Round, College, Production, Athleticism, Need Fit, Status, with Proj Pick and Act Pick as within-round pick numbers. Act Pick, Act Team, Act Round, and Value vs Rank are `XLOOKUP` formulas from player name into `_actuals` that stay blank until actuals exist. The owner has reordered this tab twice, so no code depends on those positions: `resolveTrackerColumns_` maps every field by its header label at runtime and every consumer skips rather than writing when a label is missing or duplicated. `Big Board` keys prospects by Rank in column A and Player in column B. `Player-Bios` keys player profiles by Player in column A. NFL teams use the full team name in `Players-Draft Tracker!D:D` and `_nfl_info!B:B`; `_nfl_info!C:C` holds the abbreviation and `_nfl_info!G:G` holds the division.

Fantasy league teams use `_config!F2:F13` as the display key and `_config!E2:E13` as the stable team ID. Manager, contact, optional NFL franchise, colors, logo, draft slot, active state, and notes are stored in `_config!G:P`. Fantasy settings use the rule label in `_config!S:S` as the key and the editable value in `_config!T:T` as the value.

`FantasyPros Players`, `FantasyPros Rankings`, `FantasyPros Consensus`, `FantasyPros Projections`, `FantasyPros Injuries`, and `FantasyPros News` are replace-on-success source tables. `FantasyPros Player Points` is a normalized historical fact table keyed by Season, Scoring, and Player ID. `FantasyPros History` summarizes the stored PPR facts by player with seasons, total points, average points per season, and average points per game.

## derived-relationships

Tracker player names in `Players-Draft Tracker!A:A` join to `Big Board!B:B` for position, college, board rank, NFL grade, production, and athleticism, and to `_actuals!E:E` for actual pick, team, round, and overall. Team views and analytics join `_actuals!E:E` to `Big Board!B:B` for per-pick grades and board ranks. `Team-Needs` is a manual input source for the tracker Need Fit column, but it does not alter draft results, grades, or rankings. `Team-Report!L3` and `Team-Compare!B52`, `G52`, and `L52` join to `_config!F2:F13`; manager, draft slot, and optional NFL franchise are returned with `XLOOKUP`.

## control-and-analysis

`_updates` is the live refresh control plane. `Update Log` is the immutable execution history. `Draft Rules` and Commissioner Dashboard are readiness surfaces. `Commissioner Dashboard!A24:H40` is the fantasy league rollup. `Analysis-Saved` is the saved-analysis index, written only after the Apps Script menu is installed: columns A through N hold name, type, three team selectors, five player selectors, college selector, notes, saved by, and timestamp; column O links to the analysis's values-only `Saved - <name>` snapshot tab; columns P through R track performance as reality actualizes (picks at save, players drafted now, average actual overall now). The type is detected from the active sheet, with a distinct `Fantasy Team Comparison` type for `Team-Compare!B52`, `G52`, and `L52`. `Mobile` is a generated one-column phone view in draft or season mode. `Season Forecast` derives head-to-head win probabilities, expected wins, forecast delta, champion odds, and fair betting lines from editable power ratings, actual wins, and season length, keyed to the fantasy teams in `_config!F2:G13`; edited inputs survive rebuilds by team name. `Recommendations` is a generated tab of live ranked option lists (free-agent targets, value picks, reaches, contenders, dark horses, tightest matchups) derived from the Big Board and the forecast. `Draft Optimizer` is a generated snake draft board keyed by pick number: the Player column is the league's editable pick log, and its recommendation blocks derive from Big Board availability net of recorded picks; picks and the round count survive rebuilds. Player-name cells converted by `Link player names to bios` keep the name as the cell value, so every join keyed on player names still resolves.
